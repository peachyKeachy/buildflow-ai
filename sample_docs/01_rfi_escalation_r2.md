# SYNTHETIC SAMPLE: RFI Escalation Procedure

## Urgent escalation
For a blocking RFI open more than seven days, the project engineer prepares the Cedar escalation packet within four working hours of identifying the threshold. The fictional escalation owner, project manager Mira Chen, receives the packet. Copy the superintendent and design coordinator. This is a sample communication deadline, not authorization to proceed with affected work. Revision 2 supersedes revision 1's forty-eight-hour deadline.

## Packet contents
The Cedar escalation packet contains the RFI number, exact question, affected drawing and revision, affected work area, days open, blocking impact, proposed options, and requested decision date. Attach marked-up evidence. Do not invent a cost or schedule impact; label unverified impacts as pending validation. The packet is an internal draft requiring human review.

## Routine follow-up
For a nonblocking RFI, the project engineer records a routine follow-up in the coordination log after five working days without a response. Routine follow-up does not make an RFI critical. Critical means blocking and open more than seven days. Do not use the urgent escalation deadline for submittal reviews.
