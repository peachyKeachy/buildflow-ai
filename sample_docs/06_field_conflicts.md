# SYNTHETIC SAMPLE: Field Conflicts

## Routing conflict triage
When intersecting electrical, mechanical, or plumbing service routes are discovered, pause the affected installation, photograph the conflict, verify the current coordinated drawing revisions, and identify clearance and maintenance-access requirements. The superintendent arranges a joint trade walkthrough. Submit an RFI if resolving the clash requires design interpretation. Do not cut, relocate, or conceal services without documented approval.

## BX-17 clearance hold
The fictional BX-17 coordination hold concerns a duct crossing a cable tray at grid C4. The sample coordination sketch requests 150 millimeters of maintenance clearance above the tray. The mechanical foreman and electrical foreman must verify that dimension during the joint walkthrough before proposing rerouting. This sample dimension is not an engineering instruction and must never be used for actual installation.

## Resolution evidence
Capture the agreed option, drawing revision, approving reviewer, affected trades, and release condition in the field issue record. A clash is not resolved merely because one trade proposes an alternate route. Update the coordination model only after the authorized reviewer confirms the resolution.
